Articles Overview

  • How PerpetualBooster Works (July 19, 2025) — A mathematical deep-dive into Step Size Control (forward-tracking tree search) and Generalization Control (in-split validation loss checks) that eliminate hyperparameter tuning.

  • 100x Faster Initial Training (January 22, 2025) — Benchmarking PerpetualBooster against Optuna-tuned LightGBM on California Housing (112x–148x faster) and Forest Cover Types (76x faster).

  • Better Prediction Intervals with Perpetual Learning (January 23, 2025) — Comparing Conformalized Quantile Regression (CQR) in PerpetualBooster vs. MAPIE on California Housing (35% narrower prediction intervals at 90% coverage).

  • Further Speed-Up with Continual Learning (January 24, 2025) — Demonstrating how continual learning (reset=False) reduces cumulative training time from \(O(n^2)\) to \(O(n)\) (2.6x cumulative speed-up across 11 streaming updates at identical test MSE).

  • Optimum Business Decisioning with Calibrated Probabilities (October 27, 2025) — Bridging probability calibration and expected utility on the OpenML TVS_Loan_Default dataset (+$75,500 / +1.3% higher lending profit than calibrated LightGBM).

  • Profit-Driven Lead Scoring (October 31, 2025) — Profit-driven lead scoring on the UCI Bank Marketing dataset (+1.0% higher net campaign profit than LightGBM + CalibratedClassifierCV).
